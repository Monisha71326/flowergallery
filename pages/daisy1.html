DAISY

The Daisy is a simple yet charming flowering plant belonging, to the
familyAsteraceae (the same family as sunflowers and marigolds) are
It is recognized for its white petals with a yellow central disc,
though varieties with pink or purple petals also exist.


The name "daisy" originates from “day’s eye”and because the flower
opens at sunrise and closes at sunset.
