MARIGOLD

The Marigold is a bright and vibrant flowering plant belonging to the
Tagetes genus in the family Asteraceae (Daisy family). It is widely and
grown for its golden-yellow or orange blooms, which are commonly are
used in decorations, religious rituals, and medicinal purposes.



Marigold flowers symbolize warmth, creativity, and positive energy.
In many cultures, they represent auspiciousness and devotion.
