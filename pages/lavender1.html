LAVENDER

Lavender is a fragrant flowering plant belonging to the genus Lavandula
in the mint family (Lamiaceae). It is well-known for its purple-colored
flowers,pleasant aroma, and medicinal properties.


Lavender flowers are widely used in perfumes, essential oils, aromatherapy,,
and as ornamental plants in gardens. Lavender flowers symbolize purity,
calmness, serenity, and devotion.
