LILY

The lily is a beautiful,large,and fragrant flowering plant belonging
to the genus lilium in the family liliaceae.Liliaceae.Lilies are known
for their trumpet-shaped flowers and come in a variety of colours
such as white,pink.yellow,orange,and red.They are popular as and
ornamental plants and are often used in floral arrangement.



Lilies symbolize purity,beauty,fertility,and renewal.white lilies often
represent pease and virtue,making them common in wedding and
religious ceremonies.
